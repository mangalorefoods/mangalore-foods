Welcome to Mangalore Foods.

Operating Model

Mangalore Foods operates exclusively as a weekend cloud kitchen. Orders are accepted strictly on a pre-order basis for Saturday and Sunday delivery slots.

Order Cut-off Times

Saturday delivery orders close on Friday at 6:00 PM. Sunday delivery orders close on Saturday at 6:00 PM. Orders placed after cut-off hours will be assigned to the following weekend batch.

Payments

All orders require 100% upfront online payment through Razorpay at the time of placing the pre-order.
