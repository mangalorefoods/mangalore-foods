Welcome to Mangalore Foods.

Operating Model

Mangalore Foods operates as a weekend cloud kitchen specializing in Saturday and Sunday lunch service (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM). Orders are accepted strictly on a pre-order basis.

Order Cut-off Times

Saturday lunch pre-orders close on Friday at 6:00 PM. Sunday lunch pre-orders close on Saturday at 6:00 PM. Orders received after cut-off times will be rolled over to the following weekend batch.

Payments & Dispatch

All orders require 100% upfront online payment through Razorpay during checkout. Dispatch is initiated via distance-based delivery aggregators (e.g., Rapido, Porter).
