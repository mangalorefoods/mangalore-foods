Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Mangalore Foods, accessible from our online storefront, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Mangalore Foods and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you place a pre-order via our integrated Google Form/Razorpay checkout, we collect personal information including your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address to fulfill your weekend food delivery.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your weekend food orders.
  • To send order confirmation receipts and delivery updates.
  • To distribute weekly marketing menus (only if consent is provided).

Data Security

We do not store financial or credit/debit card details on our servers. All online payments are securely processed via Razorpay.
