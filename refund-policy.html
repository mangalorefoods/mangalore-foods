Refund & Cancellation Policy

Order Cancellations

Because our food is freshly prepared based on batch pre-orders, cancellations are only accepted prior to order cut-off times (Friday 6:00 PM for Saturday orders; Saturday 6:00 PM for Sunday orders).

Refund Process

If an order is cancelled before the cut-off time, a 100% refund will be processed to the original payment source within 5–7 business days. Once food preparation has begun after cut-off hours, cancellations and refunds are not permitted.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

In the rare event of a missing item or packaging issue, please contact us immediately via WhatsApp or email at namma.mangalorefoods@gmail.com with a photo within 1 hour of delivery for resolution.
