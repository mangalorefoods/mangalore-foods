Delivery Policy

Operating Days & Delivery Window

Mangalore Foods operates exclusively as a weekend cloud kitchen. Deliveries are fulfilled on Saturdays and Sundays during our dedicated lunch slot:

  • Lunch Delivery Slot: 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM

Delivery Dispatch & Third-Party Partners

Deliveries are initiated directly from our kitchen facility depending on the customer's distance. We utilize reliable on-demand delivery services including Rapido, Porter, Swiggy Genie, or local courier partners to ensure your food arrives fresh and hot.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges are calculated dynamically based on distance and vehicle type required (two-wheeler or four-wheeler) at the time of order dispatch.

Customer Availability

Please ensure someone is available at the provided delivery address between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Because our dishes are cooked fresh to order, undelivered packages due to unreachable customers or incorrect addresses cannot be refunded.
