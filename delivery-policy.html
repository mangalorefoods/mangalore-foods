Delivery Policy

Delivery Schedule

Deliveries are fulfilled exclusively on Saturdays and Sundays during selected time windows:

  • Lunch Slot: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM
  • Dinner Slot: 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM

Customer Availability

Please ensure someone is available at the delivery address during your selected window. If the delivery driver is unable to reach you after multiple attempts, the package will be returned and no refund will be issued for perishable meals.
